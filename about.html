About Elijah

Osituwo Elijah Ayokunle is a Nigerian educator, lifelong learner, technology enthusiast, entrepreneur, and aspiring screenwriter whose journey is defined by resilience, service, continuous learning, and an unwavering commitment to personal growth.

Born into the Osituwo family in Nigeria, Elijah believes that age does not define the greatness of a determined person. Rather, character, knowledge, dedication, service, and the impact one creates are what truly matter.

His interests span education, science, technologies, entrepreneurship, financial literacy, and creative storytelling.

Education & Teaching Journey

Elijah began his teaching career after completing his secondary education. He has taught in both primary and secondary schools.

At the secondary level, he taught Mathematics, Basic Technology, and Cultural and Creative Arts, while at the primary level he taught across multiple subjects.

These experiences strengthened his passion for education and reinforced his belief that learning is one of the most powerful tools for individual and national development.

A Journey of Self-Education

Following the separation of his parents, Elijah faced financial challenges that affected his ability to immediately pursue higher education through the traditional route.

Rather than allowing these circumstances to end his educational journey, he pursued scholarships, free courses, online learning platforms, and independent study.

Through discipline, persistence, and self-directed learning, he developed knowledge and practical skills across multiple fields.

Service & Community

Elijah's commitment to serving others began early in life.

Using personal savings as a boy, he purchased basic first-aid supplies to provide free assistance to people in his community. When someone required further medical attention, he encouraged them to seek appropriate professional healthcare.

He believes that even small acts of kindness can make a meaningful difference in another person's life.

Technology, Innovation & Continuous Learning

Elijah is committed to continuous growth and has devoted himself to studying areas including:

  • Science
  • Technology
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Cybersecurity
  • Software Development
  • Engineering
  • Entrepreneurship
  • Finance

He is particularly interested in developing future-ready skills and applying technology to practical challenges. His long-term technology vision includes WELLTEC & ELICAN, an initiative he is working toward with the goal of making technology education and opportunities more accessible, particularly for young people.

Creative Vision

Elijah is interested in technology, screenwriting and storytelling.

As an aspiring screenwriter and storyteller, he seeks to develop compelling stories inspired by African history, culture, human experiences, and universal values.

Through storytelling, he hopes to educate, inspire, preserve cultural heritage, celebrate human creativity, and encourage positive social change.

Professional Direction

Guided by the principles of integrity, lifelong learning, compassion, excellence, and service, Osituwo Elijah Ayokunle is developing his professional path across education, technology, business, and the creative industries.

His long-term vision is to become a respected leader whose work empowers individuals, advances innovation, expands access to knowledge, creates meaningful opportunities, and contributes to technological and sustainable development.
