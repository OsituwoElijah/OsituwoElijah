DIGITAL IDENTITY • PERSONAL BRANDING

Smart Digital Business Identity Card

A professionally designed personal business identity card created to combine traditional business-card functionality with modern digital technology.

The card features a scannable QR code connected directly to my personal website, allowing anyone to quickly access my professional profile, projects, services, contact information, and online presence.

Key Features

  • Scannable QR Code — connects directly to my personal website.
  • 🪪 Personal Identity Card — serves as a digital and physical representation of my professional identity.
  • 💼 Business Card Functionality — provides essential contact and professional information.
  • 🌐 Social Media Integration — displays my social handles and online presence.
  • Professional Design — created with a clean, modern, and distinctive visual identity.
  • International Standard — designed with a professional presentation suitable for networking and business environments.

Purpose

The project demonstrates how a traditional business identity can be enhanced with technology, making it easier for people to connect with my professional profile and discover my work through a single QR code.

Technology & Concept: QR Code • Digital Identity • Personal Branding • Web Integration • Graphic Design
